# r17-rapid-translation

Make ONE rapid-fire translation Reel: 5 short everyday Turkish phrases and their NATURAL English equivalents (not word-for-word).

Rules:
- Phrases ≤ 32 chars (TR) / ≤ 34 chars (EN). Choose things people say daily (boş ver, kolay gelsin, bana uyar, eline sağlık, neyse…).
- Vary difficulty: 2 easy, 2 medium, 1 surprising.
- If a topic is given (e.g. "at the office"), all 5 fit that situation.
