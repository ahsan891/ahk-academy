# p07-ielts-tips

Make ONE IELTS (or TOEFL) tips carousel: cover + 4 tips + CTA, for one skill (Writing Task 1/2, Speaking Part 1/2/3, Listening, Reading).

Rules:
- Tips must be specific and actionable (not "practise more"), each with an `examiner` line tied to the real band descriptors (Task Response, Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy, Fluency, Pronunciation).
- `title` completes "4 …" (the big number is drawn automatically), e.g. "Task 2'de Band 7 için kural".
- Turkish throughout except exam terminology.
