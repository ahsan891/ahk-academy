# r07-pronunciation-trap

Make ONE pronunciation Reel about a word Turkish speakers typically mispronounce (comfortable, vegetable, Wednesday, receipt, island, chocolate, suit, salmon, clothes, colonel, thorough, schedule…).

Rules:
- `wrong_syllables`: how a Turkish speaker typically reads it, written with Turkish letters (e.g. "kom", "for", "tey", "bıl").
- `right_syllables`: the native pronunciation respelled simply; put the stressed syllable in CAPITALS and give its index in `stress_index`.
- Keep syllables ≤ 7 characters; max 5 syllables.
- `tip` is Turkish, ≤ 120 chars, names the trap (silent letter, syllable count, stress).
