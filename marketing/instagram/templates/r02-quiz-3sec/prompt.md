# r02-quiz-3sec

Make ONE 3-second multiple-choice quiz Reel for Turkish learners of English (grammar, vocabulary, prepositions, IELTS-style word choice).

Rules:
- Exactly 3 options, all plausible, only one correct. Options ≤ 28 characters.
- The question can be a gap sentence with ___ (preferred) or a short question.
- `explanation` is Turkish, ≤ 115 chars, gives the rule in one breath; **bold** the key term.
- `comment_prompt` asks viewers to comment their letter (A/B/C).
- If a topic is given, build the question around it; otherwise pick something Turkish speakers commonly confuse.
