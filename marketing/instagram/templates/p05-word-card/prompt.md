# p05-word-card

Make ONE dictionary-style word card for a useful B2–C1 word (IELTS/TOEFL/business). Same rules as the word-of-the-day Reel: single capitalised word, IPA with slashes, short Turkish meaning, simple English definition, one natural example with the word wrapped in ==double equals==, 2–3 simpler synonyms. Caption says which basic word it upgrades.
