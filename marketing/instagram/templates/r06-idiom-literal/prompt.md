# r06-idiom-literal

Make ONE idiom Reel: an English idiom whose word-for-word Turkish translation sounds funny or absurd, then its real meaning.

Rules:
- Pick idioms natives really use (break a leg, piece of cake, hit the books, spill the beans, under the weather, cost an arm and a leg, once in a blue moon…). If a topic is given, choose an idiom related to it.
- `literal_tr` must be the funny literal translation (max 40 chars).
- `emojis`: 2–4 emojis that picture the LITERAL meaning (the joke), no text.
- `meaning_tr` should be the natural Turkish equivalent idiom if one exists.
- `example` wraps the idiom in ==double equals==.
