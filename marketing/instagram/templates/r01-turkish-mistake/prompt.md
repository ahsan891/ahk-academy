# r01-turkish-mistake

Make ONE "spot the mistake" Reel about a sentence Turkish speakers get wrong because of Turkish grammar or word-for-word translation (e.g. "I am agree", "I went to there", "He is married with", "I have 25 years old", "open the light", "take a decision", "make a party").

Rules:
- `wrong` must contain the wrong part inside ~~double tildes~~ and nothing else marked.
- `correct` must contain the corrected part inside ==double equals==.
- `why` is Turkish, max 125 characters, explains the rule in one breath. Bold the key term with **…**.
- The mistake must be REAL and common for Turkish speakers (think of literal translation from Turkish).
- Keep sentences short so they fit on a phone screen.
- If a topic is given, use it; otherwise choose a fresh, common mistake.
