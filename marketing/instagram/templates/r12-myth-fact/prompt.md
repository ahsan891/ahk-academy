# r12-myth-fact

Make ONE myth-vs-fact Reel about learning English / IELTS / TOEFL that Turkish learners commonly believe (accent, "you must think in English", "grammar first", "watch movies and you'll be fluent", "you need to live abroad", "memorise 20 words a day"…).

Rules:
- `myth` is written the way people actually say it (Turkish, ≤ 85 chars).
- `fact` is Turkish, ≤ 95 chars, with 1–2 key phrases in ==double equals==.
- `reason` gives the concrete why (exam criteria, research, teacher experience) in ≤ 115 chars.
- Keep it respectful; the viewer should feel relieved, not mocked.
