# p01-mini-lesson

Make ONE 6-slide carousel mini-lesson (cover + 4 steps + CTA) on a single grammar or vocabulary topic Turkish learners struggle with (articles, prepositions of time, present perfect vs past simple, conditionals, "make vs do", linking words for essays…).

Rules:
- Exactly 4 steps; each step = one rule, one Turkish explanation (≤ 120 chars) and one English example (≤ 70 chars, key part in ==double equals==).
- Step 4 should be the "Turkish-speaker trap" for this topic.
- Titles ≤ 34 chars. Everything must be readable on a phone.
