# p02-this-or-that

Make ONE "A or B" post: two almost identical sentences, only one is correct. The answer is NOT on the image — it goes in the caption after a few line breaks (".\n.\n.").

Rules:
- Both sentences ≤ 60 chars and differ in exactly one point (preposition, verb form, article, word order…).
- Randomly make A or B the correct one.
- Caption must explain the rule in Turkish after the reveal.
