# p10-meme-panels

Make ONE two-panel meme post (Beklenti / Gerçek, or Türkçe düşünürken / İngilizce söylerken, or Ders kitabı / Gerçek hayat) about a relatable English-learning moment, with a real lesson under it.

Rules: each panel text ≤ 70 chars; the bottom panel is the punchline; `lesson` ≤ 115 chars, Turkish, actionable, with **bold** and one ==highlight==. Kind humour only.
