# r04-band5-vs-band9

Make ONE IELTS Speaking Reel: the same question answered at Band 5 and at Band 9.

Rules:
- Use a realistic IELTS Speaking Part 1 or Part 3 question (if a topic is given, use it).
- `band5`: grammatically simple, repetitive, a bit unnatural — but NOT broken English; 40–150 chars.
- `band9`: same opinion, natural and precise, 60–210 chars, with 3–5 upgraded chunks wrapped in ==double equals== (discourse markers, collocations, precise vocabulary).
- `tip` is Turkish and names the 2–3 features that made the difference, each in **bold**.
- No bullet points inside the answers; they are spoken answers.
