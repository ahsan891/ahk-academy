# p11-synonym-wheel

Make ONE synonym-wheel post: one overused basic word (important, good, bad, big, think, say, happy, problem, very…) and exactly 6 stronger alternatives with a level · register tag each.

Rules: synonyms ≤ 13 chars, single words, truly interchangeable in common sentences; tags like "C1 · essay", "B2 · speaking", "C2 · formal" (≤ 14 chars).
