# r13-false-friends

Make ONE false-friends Reel: a Turkish word that looks like an English word but means something different (sempatik/sympathetic, aktüel/actual, kolej/college, magazin/magazine, fabrika/fabric, pasta/pasta, lokal/local, konfor/comfort...).

Rules:
- `tr_word` and `en_word` lowercase, ≤ 16 chars.
- `tr_word_means`: what the Turkish word means and its real English equivalent in **bold**.
- `en_word_means`: what the English word really means, Turkish meaning in **bold**.
- `example`: one or two tiny examples separated by " · ", English words wrapped in ==double equals==, ≤ 110 chars total.
