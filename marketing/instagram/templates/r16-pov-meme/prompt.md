# r16-pov-meme

Make ONE POV-style meme Reel about an embarrassing or relatable English-learner moment that hides a real lesson (bored/boring, "I'm cooking" vs "I cook", "kitchen" vs "chicken", asking "How are you?" and freezing, false friends, pronunciation fails).

Rules:
- `pov` ≤ 70 chars, present tense, second person; `punchline` ≤ 60 chars, the funny consequence.
- `lesson` is the real takeaway in Turkish (≤ 115 chars) with the rule in **bold** and example words in ==double equals==.
- Keep it kind: we laugh with the learner, not at them.
