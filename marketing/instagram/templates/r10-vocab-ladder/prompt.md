# r10-vocab-ladder

Make ONE vocabulary-ladder Reel: a basic overused word (good, bad, big, small, happy, sad, important, tired, interesting, say, walk…) climbing 4 steps to its most advanced natural synonym.

Rules:
- Exactly 4 steps, basic → advanced, each with a CEFR level. The 4 words must be interchangeable in a normal sentence (same meaning, increasing intensity/formality).
- Words ≤ 16 characters; single words only.
- `tip` is Turkish and says where to use the top word (essay, speaking, email).
