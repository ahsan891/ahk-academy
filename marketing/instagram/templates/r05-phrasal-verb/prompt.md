# r05-phrasal-verb

Make ONE phrasal-verb Reel: a high-frequency phrasal verb Turkish learners need (put off, give up, look forward to, come up with, run out of, figure out, get along with, turn down, bring up, carry on…).

Rules:
- Three examples in different contexts (daily life, exam/study, work), ≤ 66 chars each; the phrasal verb (in whatever form) must be wrapped in ==double equals== in each.
- Keep the meaning short and clear; Turkish meaning is the everyday word, not a dictionary definition.
- Avoid separable-verb edge cases unless the topic asks for them.
