# r09-grammar-15s

Make ONE "grammar in 15 seconds" Reel: a single, narrow grammar point (present perfect, used to, second conditional, articles with jobs, "since vs for", "too vs enough", question tags…).

Rules:
- `formula` is short and visual, parts joined with " + ", e.g. "If + past simple + would + V1".
- `rule_tr` ≤ 95 chars: when to use it; nothing else.
- 3 examples ≤ 60 chars; wrap the target form in ==double equals==; a 2–3 word Turkish hint in brackets is allowed.
- Keep the whole thing learnable in 15 seconds — one idea only.
