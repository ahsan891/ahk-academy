# AHK Akademi — Lead Magnet Research (Phase 1)

Date: 2026-10-03. Scope: Turkish-speaking learners of **English** and **Arabic**, Google Turkey.

> **How to read this file.** Every claim is tagged **[E]** (evidence: a source is linked) or **[J]** (judgement: my reasoning, no hard data). I could not access Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush or Google Autocomplete from this environment (the autocomplete endpoint and several sites, including ahkademy.com and lemonacademy.co.uk, were blocked by the network proxy). **No search-volume numbers in this document are measured; none are invented.** Keywords are ranked by *evidence of demand* (what already ranks, what kinds of sites compete, what "People also ask"-style questions appear in results) and by *winnability* for a small academy.

## 2. Which lead-magnet formats convert (and why we chose interactive tools)

| Finding | Evidence / note |
|---|---|
| Quiz-style lead magnets with a **personalised result** report ~40 % opt-in on average (Interact data, vendor-reported) vs 10–20 % for static PDFs/checklists; ~65–80 % of starters finish. | [E] [Kajabi quiz guide (cites Interact)](https://kajabi.com/blog/quiz-lead-magnet-creation-guide), [Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/blog/how-to-create-a-quiz-as-a-lead-magnet/), [Segmetrics playbook (PDF)](https://segmetrics.io/guides/download/Playbook-Optin-Funnel-Quiz.pdf). **Caveat [J]:** vendor data, not independent; our own numbers will be lower until the pages are tuned. |
| A/B anecdote: an interactive quiz converted 11 % vs 1.95 % for the same content as a PDF. | [E] [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/how-do-lead-magnets-work-do-they-still-convert/) (single case) |
| Every serious competitor in Turkey offers a **free level test** as its main top-of-funnel tool (Lemon Academy "anında sonuç veren ücretsiz İngilizce seviye testi", universities' "seviye tespit sınavı", EF SET, Cambridge "Test your English", British Council placement test, Flalingo AI level test, Papora free trial lessons). | [E] [Lemon Academy](https://lemonacademy.co.uk/ingilizce-seviye-testi), [Lemon Academy (2)](https://lemonacademy.co.uk/?p=37064), [EF SET](https://efset.org/cefr), [Indigo Dergisi – Cambly/Open English/Flalingo](https://indigodergisi.com/2025/06/cambly-mi-open-english-mi-flalingo-mu/), [Papora](https://www.papora.com/tr/ingilizce-ogren/online-ucretsiz/) |
| Gaps [J]: (a) most Turkish tests are 40–80 dry grammar questions with a plain "B1" at the end – no animated, personalised result, no "what to do next"; (b) **no Turkish-language interactive Arabic tools** (alphabet trainer, goal finder) surfaced in search – the Arabic side is books, PDF lecture notes and YouTube; (c) "how long will it take" calculators exist only as blog posts (Univext, BO Enstitü), not as tools. | [E] [Univext – İngilizce öğrenmek ne kadar sürer](https://univext.com/tr/blog/384/ingilizce-ogrenmek-ne-kadar-surer), [BO Enstitü](https://boenstitu.com/blog/ingilizce-nasil-ogrenilir-hizli-ogrenme-yontemleri) |

**Decision [J]:** lead with *interactive tools that give value before asking for anything* (test, trainer, calculator), show the result ungated, and gate only the "extra" (PDF report / 7-day plan). This is both the best-converting format in the evidence and the least dark-pattern.

### 3c. Why these URL slugs
ASCII slugs without Turkish characters (`ingilizce-seviye-testi`, not `ingilizce-seviye-testi̇`) – Google handles Unicode URLs, but ASCII avoids encoding bugs on every host and in WhatsApp/Instagram link previews. [E] [Moz Q&A on Turkish characters in URLs](https://mozdev.nodebb.com/community/q/post/10500), [Ranktracker](https://www.ranktracker.com/da/blog/a-complete-guide-for-doing-seo-in-turkey/).

## 4. Technical SEO rules applied (and what NOT to expect)

| Topic | What we do | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **FAQ rich results** | Every page has a useful FAQ and valid `FAQPage` JSON-LD, **but** since Aug 2023 Google shows FAQ rich results only for authoritative government/health sites. Keep the markup (valid, helps AI/answer engines, harmless), do not expect the rich snippet. | [E] [Google: Changes to HowTo and FAQ rich results](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes) |
| **HowTo** | Deprecated – not used. | same source |
| **Quiz** | `Quiz` + `Question` markup is valid schema.org and Google's "Education Q&A" feature uses it, but that carousel is only live in English/Portuguese/Spanish/Vietnamese – not Turkish. We include `Quiz` markup for structure, not for a rich result. | [E] [Google Education Q&A](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/education-qa) |
| **Course** | The 7-day mini-course uses `Course` + `CourseInstance` (`courseMode: online`, `courseWorkload`); Google's Course *list* rich result needs ≥3 courses + carousel on one page – we provide an `ItemList` of 3 free mini-courses on the hub page so the owner can grow into it. | [E] [Google Course list](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/course), [Course info](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/course-info) |
| **Breadcrumb / Organization / WebSite** | `BreadcrumbList` on every page, `Organization` + `WebSite` on the hub (same `@id` reused). Standard. | Google structured-data gallery |
| **hreflang** | `<link rel="alternate" hreflang="tr">`, `hreflang="en"` and `x-default` on the one page that exists in two languages (English level test). Each language version lists itself and the others; `x-default` → Turkish (primary audience). | [E] [Google: localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions) |
| **Core Web Vitals** | Targets: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1 (75th percentile, mobile). Implemented by: no external JS library, self-hosted subset fonts with `font-display: swap`, explicit image dimensions, CSS-only hero animation, no layout-shifting components, no third-party scripts. | [E] [CWV thresholds](https://www.vitalsentinel.com/glossary/core-web-vitals-thresholds), [Google localized/CWV docs] |
| **Mobile** | Mobile-first (85 %+ mobile searches in Turkey). | [E] Ranktracker (above) |
| **Titles / descriptions** | Unique Turkish `<title>` (≤ 60 chars, keyword first), `<meta description>` (≤ 155 chars, includes the CTA word "ücretsiz"), canonical on every page, OG + Twitter cards with a 1200×630 brand image per page. | Standard; title tag is a confirmed (moderate) ranking factor [E] [Rankability](https://www.rankability.com/blog/title-tags-ranking-factor/) |

## 5. KVKK, ETK/İYS and WhatsApp – what the forms must do

| Requirement | Implementation in the site | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **KVKK (Law 6698) – aydınlatma yükümlülüğü**: the data subject must be *informed* (who the controller is, purpose, legal basis, recipients, rights under Art. 11) **before** data is collected. | Every form links to `/gizlilik/` (Aydınlatma Metni) and shows a one-line summary next to the checkbox. | [E] [Mailsoftly – KVKK uyumlu e-posta pazarlama](https://mailsoftly.com/tr/blog/kvkk-uyumlu-e-posta-pazarlama/), [KVKK public announcement analysis](https://www.hukukihaber.net/ucuncu-kisilerden-elde-edilen-verilerle-pazarlama-yapilmasina-iliskin-kvkk-duyurusunun-hukuki-analizi/amp) |
| **Açık rıza** must be specific, informed and freely given; it cannot be a pre-ticked box or bundled with the service. | Checkbox 1 (required, unticked): consent to processing name/e-mail/phone *to deliver the requested result/resource*. Checkbox 2 (optional, unticked): **ticari elektronik ileti** consent (e-mail/SMS/WhatsApp promotions). Separate boxes → separate purposes. | [E] [Paraşüt – ticari elektronik ileti açık rıza formu](https://www.parasut.com/yeni-donem-ticari-elektronik-ileti-acik-riza-formu), [Milliyet – ticari ileti bilgilendirme](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/elektronik-ticari-ileti-kurallari-ve-bilgilendirme-metni) |
| **ETK (Law 6563) + İYS**: any promotional e-mail/SMS/WhatsApp needs prior consent and the consent must be registered in **İYS (iys.org.tr)**; transactional messages (sending the result the person asked for) do not. | Site stores the two consents with timestamp + page URL in the payload (`consents: {kvkk, marketing}`), so the owner can upload marketing consents to İYS. Transactional delivery of the requested PDF/result is fine without box 2. | [E] [Hukukçular Evi – 6563 / İYS](https://hukukcularevi.com/ticari-elektronik-ileti-iys-izin-6563-reklam/), [CottGroup – İYS](https://cottgroup.com/tr/mevzuat/item/ileti-yonetim-sistemi-ve-yeni-uygulamalar) |
| WhatsApp as channel | A `wa.me` deep link with a pre-filled Turkish message is offered on every result screen (user-initiated conversation – no consent problem). Outbound WhatsApp marketing still needs box 2 + İYS. | [E] WhatsApp usage data §1; [ChatDaddy – WhatsApp marketing Turkey](https://chatdaddy.tech/blog/whatsapp-marketing-turkey) (vendor) |

**[J] The privacy notice I drafted is a starting point and must be reviewed by a Turkish lawyer** (controller identity, VERBİS registration need, retention periods, processors such as the e-mail/CRM tool).

### Honest limitations
* No measured search volumes (tooling blocked). Before investing in paid promotion, verify the clusters in Google Search Console (free) after 4–8 weeks, or in a keyword tool.
* Conversion-rate figures above are vendor-published; treat 10–20 % opt-in as a realistic first target for these pages, not 40 %.
* Rich results for FAQ/Quiz will most likely **not** appear for a Turkish academy site; the markup is still correct and future-proof.
