\section*{Speaking Session: Relationships}
\onehalfspacing
This speaking session is designed to spark meaningful conversations about relationships, both familial and friendly. Below are questions for two students to ask each other, encouraging reflection and connection.

\begin{wrapfigure}{r}{0.4\textwidth}
    \centering
    \vspace{-10pt}
    \includegraphics[width=0.38\textwidth]{/family.jpg} % Placeholder image
    \caption{\small Family and Friends}
\end{wrapfigure}

\subsection*{Student A – Questions for Student B}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Who is the person you are closest to in your family? Why?} \\
    Reflect on the bond you share and what makes this relationship special.
    \item \textbf{Do you find it easy to make new friends? Why or why not?} \\
    Consider your personality and experiences in forming new connections.
    \item \textbf{What qualities do you look for in a good friend?} \\
    Think about traits like trust, humor, or loyalty that matter to you.
    \item \textbf{How do you usually celebrate special occasions with family or friends?} \\
    Share traditions or memorable moments from celebrations.
    \item \textbf{Do you think long-distance relationships can work? Why or why not?} \\
    Explore the challenges and possibilities of maintaining connections across distances.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Student B – Questions for Student A}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Who gives you the best advice in your life?} \\
    Describe why their advice resonates with you.
    \item \textbf{Do you prefer having many friends or just a few close ones? Why?} \\
    Discuss your approach to friendships and what feels right for you.
    \item \textbf{What do you do when you have a disagreement with a friend or family member?} \\
    Share strategies for resolving conflicts or maintaining harmony.
    \item \textbf{How has technology (like social media) changed relationships today?} \\
    Consider the positive and negative impacts of technology on connections.
    \item \textbf{Do you think friends are more important than family? Why or why not?} \\
    Reflect on the roles friends and family play in your life.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Tips for a Great Discussion}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Listen Actively:} Pay attention to your partner's responses and ask follow-up questions.
    \item \textbf{Be Respectful:} Value differing perspectives and experiences.
    \item \textbf{Share Honestly:} Open up about your thoughts to deepen the conversation.
\end{itemize}
