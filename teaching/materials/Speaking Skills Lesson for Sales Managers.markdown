# Speaking Skills Lesson for Sales Managers (15 minutes)
**Focus**: Improve fluency and charisma in storytelling for professional and casual settings, using simple Business English suitable for a B1 level. This lesson teaches the "Story Arc" technique, applies it through a sales-related exercise, and ends with video practice.

## Step 1 (5 minutes) - Learn the Technique
Understand the **Story Arc** method to tell engaging stories:  

- **Hook**: Start with a surprising or clear opener to grab attention.  
- **Build-up**: Describe the challenge and actions using simple Business English (e.g., "We had a big problem with a client’s order").  
- **Climax**: Share the key solution or exciting moment.  
- **Resolution**: End with a positive result and a lesson (e.g., "This built trust and improved our sales").

**Example Story**:  
- **Hook**: Imagine losing a major client because of a delayed appliance delivery!  
- **Build-up**: The client was upset, so we quickly checked the supply chain issues.  
- **Climax**: We offered a discount and expedited shipping to save the deal.  
- **Resolution**: The client stayed with us, and we learned to prioritize fast communication.

**5 Hook Sentences**  
Simple openers to grab attention, based on home appliance sales scenarios:  
- Picture a client canceling a big order because of a late delivery!  
- What if one wrong product description lost a loyal customer?  
- Imagine a customer upset because their new fridge arrived damaged!  
- Ever had a sale almost fail due to a stock shortage?  
- Think about a major deal at risk because of a pricing mistake.

**5 Build-Up Sentences**  
Describe challenges and actions with easy Business English:  
- The client was angry about a delay, so we investigated the shipping problem.  
- A mix-up in the order caused confusion, and our team worked to fix it fast.  
- Time was tight, so we contacted the warehouse to find a solution.  
- The appliance had a defect, leading to a quick team effort to replace it.  
- A pricing error upset a client, so we reviewed our process to resolve it.

**5 Climax Sentences**  
Highlight the exciting solution or turning point:  
- Then we offered a free upgrade, and the client agreed to stay!  
- Our fast delivery of a replacement appliance saved the sale.  
- Suddenly, a personal call to the client turned their frustration into trust.  
- We found extra stock in another warehouse, securing the deal in one day!  
- The new discount we offered closed the sale instantly.

**5 Resolution Sentences**  
End with a positive result and lesson using clear Business English:  
- In the end, we kept the client and increased sales by 10% with better service.  
- The deal succeeded, and we learned to double-check orders for accuracy.  
- Our solution built stronger client trust and improved our reputation.  
- We closed the sale on time, gaining skills in quick problem-solving.  
- The fix worked, and we strengthened teamwork for future sales.

## Step 2 (5 minutes) - Solve and Apply
Choose a recent sales story (e.g., handling a client complaint or closing a big appliance deal). Map it to the Story Arc by writing 1-2 sentences for each part (Hook, Build-up, Climax, Resolution). Use simple Business English like "big issue," "team effort," or "great result." This organizes your story for clear and confident delivery.  
**Example**:  
- **Hook**: A top client threatened to switch brands over a faulty washing machine!  
- **Build-up**: We listened to their concerns and checked the product issue quickly.  
- **Climax**: We replaced the machine and added a free warranty in one day.  
- **Resolution**: The client was thrilled, and we learned to act fast to keep trust.

## Step 3 (5 minutes) - Record and Send Video
Tell your story aloud using the Story Arc. Aim for charisma with a clear voice, changing speed for excitement, and positive energy. Record a 1-2 minute video as if sharing with a colleague. Watch it to check your fluency and energy, then send it for feedback.
